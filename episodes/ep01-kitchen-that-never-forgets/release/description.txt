Priya pays ₹200 for a train ticket. Her app says "Payment successful." One millisecond later, the power goes out. Is her money safe?

This animated film follows that one payment through a computer, told as one night in a restaurant kitchen. The operating system runs the building. PostgreSQL is the catering staff working inside it. By the end you'll know what really happens between tapping Pay and seeing "done", and why the money survives the blackout.
